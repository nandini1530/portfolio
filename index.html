Leadership Principles

How I lead

My examples mapped to Amazon's Leadership Principles. Tap "View Definition" to flip.

Ownership

Owned end-to-end delivery of critical IN payment flows, taking accountability well beyond my immediate scope and building the automation and governance roadmap to keep them reliable.
Leaders are owners. They think long term and don't sacrifice long-term value for short-term results. They act on behalf of the entire company, beyond just their own team.

Deliver Results

Delivered multiple payments programs on schedule with high reliability, rising to the occasion despite shifting requirements and tight regulatory timelines.
Leaders focus on the key inputs for their business and deliver them with the right quality and in a timely fashion. Despite setbacks, they rise to the occasion and never settle.

Invent and Simplify

Streamlined delivery governance with clearer planning cadences and dashboards, simplifying how multiple squads coordinate on shared payment goals.
Leaders expect and require innovation and invention from their teams and always find ways to simplify. They are externally aware and not limited by "not invented here."

Dive Deep

Investigated root causes behind transaction failures and operational incidents, staying connected to the details and auditing metrics to drive measurable improvements.
Leaders operate at all levels, stay connected to the details, audit frequently, and are skeptical when metrics and anecdote differ. No task is beneath them.

Earn Trust

Built trust across product, engineering, risk, and compliance through transparent communication, tight feedback loops, and consistent follow-through on commitments.
Leaders listen attentively, speak candidly, and treat others respectfully. They are vocally self-critical, benchmark themselves against the best, and are right a lot.

Learn and Be Curious

Continuously explore new tools and payment technologies, leveraging data and automation to improve how programs are planned, tracked, and reported.
Leaders are never done learning and always seek to improve themselves. They are curious about new possibilities and act to explore them.
